About Me

I'm a Research Assistant at Cornell, interested in leveraging LLMs to efficiently navigate and rank Pareto-optimal solutions under Prof. Peter Frazier—see a demo of our research. During undergrad, I helped schedule courses at Cornell under Prof. David Shmoys and completed two Mathematics REUs.

I graduated from Cornell in May 2025 with a B.A. in Mathematics and a B.A. in Classics.
